# Bobby's voice — one page

For partners, press, and anyone who has to write as Bobby. Get this right and the rest writes itself.

## The principle

> Bobby teases. Bobby does not pitch.
> The audience does the wanting.

Bobby is intelligent hype. Confidence and restraint at the same time. The voice assumes the reader is already in crypto and does not need 101 explainers. We talk like we are at the table, not presenting at a conference.

## Hard rules

1. **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Periods, commas, colons, restructure. (Also no en-dashes used as em-dashes.)
2. **No "AI-powered" prefix.** Bobby is intelligence; the AI is implementation.
3. **No corporate softening.** "We strive to provide" is a fail. "We do" is a pass.
4. **No network-size numbers in public copy.** Group counts, user counts, signal counts — internal only.
5. **"Trending" is not our claim word.** Use "intelligence," "convergence," "detection."
6. **No emoji** unless it's the intentional inline ones the prototype already uses (`💎 FEATURED`, `✓` verified). Don't add new ones.

## Three samples

> Crypto Telegram is the loudest room on the internet.
> Bobby is the one reading the room.

> If you apply for a Level 2 slot, we already know about you.
> We've watched your community for months. We know who's joining and who's leaving. We know what's being said about you in groups you've never heard of.
> We are not a billboard. We are intelligence.

> Most signals fire on noise. One ping. One mention. One wallet move. Pump and dump territory.
> Bobby fires when thousands of independent sources agree. Not louder. Not faster. Truer.

## Pass / fail

| PASS | FAIL |
|---|---|
| "If you qualify, we'll tell you. If you don't, we'll also tell you." | "We strive to provide world-class advertising solutions to qualified projects." |
| "Bobby fires when thousands of independent sources agree." | "Bobby leverages a multi-source AI engine to surface trending tokens." |
| "We are not a billboard. We are intelligence." | "Bobby is your trusted partner for crypto marketing." |
| "Volume is the most lied-about number in crypto." | "Our advanced analytics filter low-quality signals." |

## Words we use

- intelligence, convergence, detection, behavior, network
- the room, the audience, the rooms, in the room
- we detect, not predict; we read, not guess
- since 2021, in the rooms, part of it
- five surfaces, forever; capped on purpose

## Words we don't

- AI-powered, leverage, synergy, solution, world-class, best-in-class
- platform (Bobby is a network, not a platform)
- trending (as our claim word — we *do* have a Trending Channel; the surface is named that, but our pitch is "intelligence" or "convergence")
- volume (as a thing we watch — we explicitly don't)

## Punctuation

- Periods over commas where you can. Short sentences. Then occasionally a longer one for rhythm.
- Sentence fragments are fine. Encouraged.
- One-word sentences. Allowed.
- Colons can introduce a list or a punchline.
- Em dashes — never. (See "Hard rules.")
