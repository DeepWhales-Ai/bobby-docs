## The principle

> Bobby teases. Bobby does not pitch.
> The audience does the wanting.

Bobby is intelligent hype. Confidence and restraint at the same time. The voice assumes the reader is already in crypto and does not need 101 explainers. We talk like we are at the table, not presenting at a conference.

## Three samples

> Crypto Telegram is the loudest room on the internet.
> Bobby is the one reading the room.

> If you apply for a Level 2 slot, we already know about you.
> We've watched your community for months. We know who's joining and who's leaving. We know what's being said about you in groups you've never heard of.
> We are not a billboard. We are intelligence.

> Most signals fire on noise. One ping. One mention. One wallet move. Pump and dump territory.
> Bobby fires when thousands of independent sources agree. Not louder. Not faster. Truer.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes. Periods, commas, colons, restructure.
- No "AI-powered" prefix. Bobby is intelligence. The implementation is implementation.
- No corporate softening. "We strive to provide" is a fail. "We do" is a pass.
- No network-size numbers in public copy.
- "Trending" is not our claim word. Use "intelligence," "convergence," "detection."

## Pass and fail

| PASS | FAIL |
|---|---|
| "If you qualify, we'll tell you. If you don't, we'll also tell you." | "We strive to provide world-class advertising solutions to qualified projects." |
| "Bobby fires when thousands of independent sources agree." | "Bobby is your trusted partner for crypto marketing solutions." |
