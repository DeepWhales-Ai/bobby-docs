## Usage rules

- The bull may appear at section heads, in callouts, on landing pages.
- Maintain breathing room. The bull is punctuation, not wallpaper.
- Lime is the only accent. Do not recolor the bull.
- The bull never appears next to other mascots or character art.
- Do not animate the bull beyond a small idle.
