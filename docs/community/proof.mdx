## Epic events

A handful of thresholds qualify as Epic and get a sticky line above the rolling feed. The Epic line stays until a bigger Epic replaces it.

- 500 or more boosts on a single token in one week.
- 1000% from entry while still trending.
- Holding #1 on The Board for an extended run.
- $1M or more in daily volume while a token is on The Board.
- All time records: most boosts, longest streak at #1, biggest single week climb.
