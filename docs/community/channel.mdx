## What a BOOST is

One tap. Free. The verb the network runs on. Every buy alert across every Bobby group has a BOOST button. Tapping it pushes the token up The Board and counts toward The League. No payment. No threshold. No gate. **Boosted tokens trend first.**

## How to follow

Open the channel link. Tap *Join*. The pinned messages appear at the top. Notifications are off by default. Bobby does not push to channel followers. The feed is there when you open it.
