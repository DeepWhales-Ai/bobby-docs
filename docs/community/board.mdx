## What weights it

Three things weight the Board. Nothing else.

**Velocity.** Fresh momentum wins. Old hype fades. A boost five minutes ago counts more than a boost five hours ago. Tap farms that loaded up overnight and went quiet drop off. Real communities boosting in real time climb.

**Quality.** Unique boosters beat tap farms. The score rewards how many different people boosted, not just how many times. A hundred boosts from one account land lower than ten boosts from ten communities. Bots lose to humans by design.

**Intelligence.** Bobby sees what is moving across the network. The Board feels it. Every boost gets weighted by what Bobby is already seeing across crypto Telegram. Quiet builds with real activity rise. Empty noise with no signal sinks. The Board is not a popularity contest. It is a signal.

## Getting on the Board

A token earns a seat three ways. Three unique boosters in six hours. Or a hundred thousand dollar market cap. Or a paid slot. Tokens with nothing behind them stay off.

Boosts are capped. Three per alert. Seventy-five per day. Caps stop spam, not communities.
