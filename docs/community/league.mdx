## How The League works

Two parallel leaderboards. One action feeds both. A user taps the BOOST button on a buy alert in their group. Their personal score goes up. Their group's aggregate score goes up. Their friends see them climbing. Their group sees the room climbing. The competition runs sideways across communities that have never spoken to each other.

## The rules

- **Boosts only.** No volume weight. No price action. No comment count. The League is one input, scored cleanly.
- **One tap, two scores.** Every tap moves the user board AND the group board. There is no separate vote.
- **Human verified.** Boosts come from Telegram users in real groups. Bots cannot fake without humans noticing.
- **No gaming the cap.** The same patterns that get a project disqualified for Level 2 disqualify a group from The League.
- **Weekly reset.** The race restarts every week. Last week becomes archive.

## What a BOOST is

One tap. Free. The verb the network runs on.

Every buy alert across every Bobby group has a BOOST button. Tapping it pushes the token up The Board and counts toward The League. No payment. No threshold. No gate.

**Boosted tokens trend first.**
