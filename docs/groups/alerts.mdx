## What you can tune

- **Threshold.** How loud a convergence event has to be before Bobby pings.
- **Quiet hours.** Bobby holds non-urgent pings for the next window.
- **Format.** Compact, full, or with socials inline.
- **Categories.** Mute holder behavior, sentiment shifts, listing events. Independently.

<TGMsg from="you" code="/alerts threshold=high quiet=22:00-08:00 format=full" />

## What you cannot tune. And why.

Bobby will not let you tune the methodology. You cannot weight a single source. You cannot turn convergence into a single-ping firehose. You cannot raise the floor so the threshold passes nothing. Those are not features. They are sabotage of the room's trust.

> If your members learn to ignore Bobby's pings, the product breaks.
> So we draw the line where the line keeps the product working.

## Sensible defaults

If you do nothing, Bobby works. Defaults are tuned per-community based on size, chain, and engagement. You can override anything in the table above. You probably do not need to.
