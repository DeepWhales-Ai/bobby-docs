## Alerts that earn the ping

Bobby does not ping for everything. A ping is a promise. When Bobby fires an alert in your group, your members learn to read it. Over time the signal compounds. Other bots ping for noise. Bobby pings for convergence.

## Intelligence on demand

Your members can ask Bobby. He answers from the same brain that powers the alerts. No methodology reveal, no theatre. Just what Bobby sees, in the room you are already in.

## Customization that respects the room

Set the portal. Set your socials. Tune the alert thresholds. Turn off what does not fit your community. Bobby never overrides admin. The room is yours. Bobby is the lens.

<Tip>
**Bobby never spams.** The room's trust is the only product Bobby cannot replace. If a setting would erode it, the setting does not exist.
</Tip>
