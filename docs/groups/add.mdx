<Info>
**Before you start.** You need to be the admin of the Telegram group. You need the token contract address. That is the entire prerequisite list.
</Info>

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a DM with Bobby">
    Find `@BobbyBuyBot` on Telegram. Start a conversation.

    <TGMsg from="you" code="/add" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the contract address">
    Bobby auto-detects chain, ticker, and listing. No form. No upload.

    <TGMsg from="you" code="0x6982508145454ce325ddbe47a25d4ec3d2311933" />
    <TGMsg bot text="Detected $PEPE on Ethereum. Mainnet. Continue?" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the group">
    Bobby lists the groups where you are admin and he can join. Pick one. He joins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Bobby pins a welcome message. He goes on duty. He will not ping until he has something to say.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after

Bobby quietly indexes your community. Within hours he has a baseline. Within a day he is firing alerts on convergence events that matter for your token. You do not have to do anything. The room is the room.
