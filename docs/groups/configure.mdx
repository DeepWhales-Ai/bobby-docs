## Set the portal

Tell Bobby where members should land when they tap the portal button on his alerts.

<TGMsg from="you" code="/set_portal https://yourproject.com" />

## Set the socials

X, Telegram channels, websites, dexscreener. Bobby renders them in his info panel and in his alerts. Render rules are below.

<TGMsg from="you" code="/set_socials x=BobbyBuyBot tg=BobbyBuyBot web=bobby.bot" />

<Info>
**The render flag.** Some communities want the socials inline on every Bobby alert. Some prefer Bobby clean. Toggle `BOBBY_SOCIALS_RENDER` on or off. When on, your socials render under every alert. When off, they only show in the info panel.
</Info>

## Permissions Bobby needs

| Permission | Required? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Send messages | Required | Bobby needs to be able to ping. |
| Pin messages | Optional | For the welcome pin and major convergence alerts. |
| Delete | Never asked for | Bobby does not moderate. |
| Admin | Required | Bobby needs admin to read group activity, post alerts, and manage the room. Bobby never moderates members or deletes content. |

Bobby is admin-permissioned, not a moderator. The admin role is the floor Telegram requires for Bobby to do his job. Moderation is not in his job description.
