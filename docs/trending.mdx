# 02 · How it works

Three things weight the board. Nothing else.

### Velocity

Fresh momentum wins. Old hype fades.

A boost five minutes ago counts more than a boost five hours ago. Tap farms that loaded up overnight and went quiet drop off. Real communities boosting in real time climb.

### Quality

Unique boosters beat tap farms.

The score rewards how many different people boosted, not just how many times. A hundred boosts from one account land lower than ten boosts from ten communities. Bots lose to humans by design.

### Intelligence

Bobby sees what's moving across the network. The board feels it.

Every boost gets weighted by what Bobby's already seeing across crypto Telegram. Quiet builds with real activity rise. Empty noise with no signal sinks. The board is not a popularity contest. It is a signal.
