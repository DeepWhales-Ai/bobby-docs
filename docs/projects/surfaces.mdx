## Why we capped at five

Every surface added is attention taken from the others. A sixth surface would not be a sixth way to win. It would be the four percent the existing five surfaces lose.

The cap is also the reason Bobby can charge what Bobby charges. The audience trusts the surface because the surface is rare. Rarity is the price.
