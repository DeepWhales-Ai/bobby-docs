## The qualification check

One automated check, run at the DM. Pass or fail. Binary. No human review, no appeals, no negotiation. The check resolves in about ten seconds. You will know on the spot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="01 · Age">
    Contract deployed at least 7 days ago. Listed on aggregator (DexScreener or DexTools) at least 3 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="02 · Listings">
    Active on CoinMarketCap or CoinGecko.
  </Step>
  <Step title="03 · Liquidity">
    Current liquidity at least $50K. Locked or burned with at least 90 days remaining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="04 · Safety">
    Blockaid scan clean. Sellable (honeypot pass). No dangerous owner functions: unrestricted mint, blacklist, extreme fee mutability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="05 · Trading health">
    24h volume at least $10K. Holder count at least 100. No single wallet over 25% of supply, excluding LP and burn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All criteria must pass.
