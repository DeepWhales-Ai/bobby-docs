## What it is

Bobby is not only a network. Bobby is the people the network found. The Lobby is the room where they meet. Crypto Telegram is fragmented by design. Every project, every chain, every meta has its own group, its own dialect, its own drama. Bobby has been inside all of it for five years. The Lobby is what happens when the listener finally introduces everyone.

It is a Telegram community. It is a daily room. It is the audience. Members talk to each other. Bobby talks to members. Convergence events get discussed there as they fire. New projects get reality-checked there before anywhere else. The Lobby is the live texture of the data.

## Who is in the Lobby

- The audience Bobby has earned trust with since 2021. Group admins. Power users. Project founders who have earned a seat.
- People from communities that have never spoken to each other before, in one room.
- Bobby. Always there. Reading the room. Replying when it matters.
