## What it is

Bobby is not only a network. Bobby is the people the network found. The Lobby is the room where they meet. Crypto Telegram is fragmented by design. Every project, every chain, every meta has its own group, its own dialect, its own drama. Bobby has been inside all of it for years. The Lobby is what happens when the listener finally introduces everyone.

It is a Telegram community. It is a daily room. It is the audience. Members talk to each other. Bobby talks to members. Convergence events get discussed there as they fire. New projects get reality-checked there before anywhere else. The Lobby is the live texture of the data.

## Who is in the Lobby

- The audience Bobby has earned trust with over the years. Group admins. Power users. Project founders who have earned a seat.
- People from communities that have never spoken to each other before, in one room.
- Bobby. Always there. Reading the room. Replying when it matters.

## Why this matters

Most "communities" in crypto are funnels with a chat tab. The Lobby is the opposite. The community came first. The network was built by being part of it. The product is the trust we earned inside those rooms over years. Putting that audience together without giving up the listening is the only thing Bobby can do that no one else can.

## Bobby on X

Most of Bobby's intelligence happens inside Telegram. That makes it invisible to anyone who is not already there. Bobby X is the public face. It is where the network speaks for itself, in front of the people who do not live in Telegram.

It carries public callouts on convergence events worth seeing. It gives editorial framing to the recommendations that matter. It is the same voice that lands in the rooms, out where everyone can read it. The public surface works because it stays restrained. It says less, so what it says counts.

## House rules

- Real conversation. No shilling without context. No raid behavior.
- Bobby's logic decides what gets surfaced inside. Not personalities. Not paid placement disguised as conversation.
- Disagreement is fine. Performance is not.
- If the room is loud, that means the signal is loud. We do not quiet the room.
